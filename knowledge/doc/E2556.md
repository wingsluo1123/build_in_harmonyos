- **类别**: ohos, headers, conan, packaging
- **作者**: unknown

## 推荐方案
**方法**: package-default-user-config-header-from-template

**说明**: |

**修复命令**:
```
copy(self, "microrl_user_config_template.h", src=..., dst=os.path.join(self.package_folder, "include", "microrl")) + cp 为 microrl_user_config.h（见 conanfile.py package()）
```
