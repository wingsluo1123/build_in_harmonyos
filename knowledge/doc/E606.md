# E606: JDK 17.0.13+ 补丁版本与旧 Maven 构建插件不兼容：maven-bundle-plugin 4.2.1 ConcurrentModificationException（bnd/ZipFile）+ javadoc doclint heading-sequence 报错，插件就地升 5.1.9 + doclint:none 一类修复

## 基本信息
- **严重度**: 2
- **类别**: portability, java, jdk, maven
- **作者**: unknown

## 错误特征
```
"ConcurrentModificationException.*ZipFile|java.util.zip.ZipFile|error: heading used out of sequence: <H[0-9]>"
```
